How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Ventilate with the bathroom exhaust fan while showering and afterward to protect new drywall.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • When kitchens, bathrooms or laundry rooms are rebuilt, choose backer board and moisture resistant drywall.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Yonkers

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
