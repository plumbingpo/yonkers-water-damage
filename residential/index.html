Water Extraction

Home Water Extraction and Removal

Storm flooding, appliance failures, and burst pipes all put water where it should not be, and having a trained crew remove it is the quickest, safest fix. Yonkers Restoration provides expert residential water extraction throughout Yonkers, handling everything from minor leaks to full-scale flooding. Every extraction includes a thorough damage assessment, industrial-grade water removal, complete moisture mapping, content protection, and detailed documentation for your insurance claim.

Standing water poses a serious risk to your family, your home, and your belongings. Hardwood starts to cup, wiring begins to corrode, and drywall soaks up water and swells, all in a matter of hours. Wait 24 to 48 hours and mold is very likely to follow. Our advice is simple: pick up the phone as soon as you find water pooling indoors, no matter how little there is. Our technicians can assess the extent of the damage and begin extraction before secondary damage sets in.

We also handle water damage caused by hidden leaks that have been ongoing for days or weeks. A slow leak behind a wall or under a floor can cause extensive structural damage before you notice any visible signs. With professional moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras, we find the wet spots you cannot see and confirm they dry out fully.

Drying Services

Moisture Monitoring and Structural Drying

After water gets in, nothing matters more for the long-term health of your house than drying the structure all the way through. Proper drying eliminates hidden moisture from walls, subfloors, and cavities, prevents mold growth, and restores your home's structural integrity. At Yonkers Restoration, every drying project follows IICRC S500 standards with daily moisture monitoring and documentation.

Our residential drying setup covers high-velocity air movers, commercial dehumidifiers, specialty systems made for wall cavities and hardwood floors, plus thermal imaging to keep verifying moisture as we go. No two houses dry the same way, so we build the plan around your layout, your building materials, and how bad the damage is.

Professional drying goes much further than most people expect. For weeks after the surface looks dry, water held under the flooring, in the framing, and behind walls can keep rotting wood and growing mold. Our team checks moisture each day and only signs off once every measurement matches what it was before the loss. Most residential drying projects take 3 to 5 days depending on the extent of damage.

Mold Prevention

Mold Prevention and Remediation for Homes

Within 24 to 48 hours of getting wet, materials can start to grow mold, which spreads quickly and can put your family's health at serious risk. Yonkers Restoration provides professional mold prevention as part of every water damage restoration project, as well as standalone mold remediation for existing growth. Our IICRC-certified technicians follow EPA and IICRC S520 protocols for safe, thorough mold treatment.

Preventive mold treatment belongs in every restoration job, and we recommend it each time. It works better and costs less to apply antimicrobials while the house is drying than to deal with established mold later. Where growth has already started, seen or suspected after a past water problem, our service covers testing, containing the area, removing the mold, and a follow-up verification that the home is safe.

Sewer Backups

Sewage Backup Cleanup for Homeowners

Few kinds of water damage are as dangerous to a household as a sewage backup. The health risks are serious because Category 3 water, also called black water, carries bacteria and viruses along with other pathogens. Yonkers Restoration technicians are specially trained and equipped to handle sewage cleanup safely, following strict IICRC protocols for contaminated water restoration.

The work runs in six steps: sealing off the area, safely pumping out the sewage, hauling away materials beyond saving, antimicrobial cleaning, full structural drying, and a final test confirming the house is safe. One team does the whole job so that your family is back in a clean and healthy house without delay.
