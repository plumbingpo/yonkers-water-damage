The HOA programs at Yonkers Restoration cover plumbing inspections aimed at prevention, common-area moisture monitoring, emergency coordination with boards and property managers, mold remediation in each affected unit, fast extraction, and structural drying.

Prevention and response plans are developed together with your board, property manager, and maintenance committee so they protect the community and stay within budget. Moisture Monitoring

Commercial Water Damage Risk Assessment and Prevention

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions on their properties. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. A Yonkers Restoration risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

An assessment does two jobs: finding vulnerabilities protects people and the property, and the record of due diligence can protect you legally should something happen. Insurance companies and courts tend to favor a property owner who acts on expert recommendations and has inspections done regularly by professionals. Every commercial property should have an annual moisture assessment, and high-risk areas, including aging plumbing systems, basements, and mechanical rooms, should be inspected more often.
