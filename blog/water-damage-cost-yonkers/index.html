How Much Does Water Damage Cost in Yonkers?

Home / Blog / How Much Does Water Damage Cost in Yonkers?

The Quick Answer

Typical Yonkers water damage projects cost from $1,500 up to $10,000 or more. A minor job involving clean water may cost less than roughly $1,500. Major flooding or a sewage backup that needs rebuilding can run well above $10,000.

These are typical national ranges, not a quote. Pricing in Yonkers, NY depends on local wages and the going rate for materials, so your number may differ. The only way to get an exact figure is an on-site inspection with moisture readings.

Pricing by Category of Water

Water type is normally the starting point when a restoration job is priced. Contamination level places every loss in one of three industry categories. A job with dirty water calls for extra protective gear, more disinfecting and more material taken out.

Nationally, the category puts cleanup at about $3 to $7.50 for each square foot. The table below shows common rates.

CategoryWhat it isTypical cost per sq ft
Category 1 (clean water)Water heater tanks, faucets and supply linesAbout $3 to $4
Category 2, gray waterWashing machine or dishwasher discharge, sump pump failuresAbout $4 to $6.50
Category 3 (black water)Sewage, river flooding, storm surgeAbout $7 to $7.50

The category is not fixed. Leave clean water standing a couple of days and bacteria grow, so it gets handled as black or gray water. That is one reason waiting costs money.

What Affects the Cost Most

Two homes with the same broken pipe can get very different bills. When a job is priced, the contractor considers the following.

  • Area affected. A larger wet area takes extra crew hours, additional machines and a longer stay.
  • Water category. Jobs with clean water cost the least. Sewage is the most expensive.
  • How long it sat. Water spreads into subfloors and wall cavities over time. A leak found in an hour costs far less than one found after a weekend away.
  • Materials. Drying goes smoothly on tile and on concrete. Special drying or outright replacement is common for drywall, hardwood, insulation and carpet pad.
  • Mold. Existing growth means remediation work, including containment and filtered air, becomes part of the job.
  • Reconstruction. One part of the bill is drying. Replacing drywall, flooring, cabinets and paint afterward is a separate one, and on large jobs it is often the bigger of the two.

Estimates usually cover a pair of phases, extraction of the water and then drying of the structure. Drying usually takes 3 to 5 days.

Air movers and dehumidifiers are often billed by the day. Three days of drying is cheaper than six.

Price Ranges by Type of Job

These are typical national ranges for the jobs we see most often in Yonkers. Treat them as a starting point, not a price.

  • Burst pipe. Cleaning up and drying out often runs $1,500 to $5,000, with repair of the burst pipe billed in addition. Expect more if it ran for hours or soaked more than one floor.
  • Appliance leak. Catch a leaking water heater, washing machine or dishwasher early and the bill is often $1,000 to $4,000. Slow leaks under cabinets cost more because of hidden damage.
  • Basement flood. Depth, where the water came from and whether the room is finished put most jobs at $2,000 to $10,000 or more. Finished basements land near the top of the range for basement water removal.
  • Sewage backup. A common range is $3,000 to $10,000 or higher. Because sewage cleanup falls under Category 3, crews disinfect the whole area and take out porous materials.
  • Roof leak. Without the roof repair itself, drying and fixing walls, insulation and ceilings is often $1,000 to $5,000.

Will Insurance Pay for Water Damage in Yonkers?

It depends on the cause and on your policy. Standard homeowners policies often cover damage that is sudden and accidental, such as a pipe that bursts or a supply line that fails without warning.

Gradual leaks are usually not covered. An insurer may treat a fitting that dripped for months as a maintenance problem. Unless you have a separate flood policy, water that enters from outside, like storm surge or a rising river, is usually excluded. A backed-up sewer often takes an added endorsement.

Do not guess: look at your policy documents or ask the agent who sold them. Your deductible also matters. On a small job, the repair may cost less than the deductible, and a claim may not make sense.

Ways to Lower the Cost

How much you pay for water damage is largely settled within a day or two. You can change the outcome with a handful of easy steps.

  • Act fast. Until someone removes it, water continues to spread. Every hour adds wet material.
  • Shut off the water. Learn the location of the main valve ahead of time, and turn it off the moment a leak shows up.
  • Document everything. Before moving or discarding anything, record it in photos and on video. Keep receipts.
  • Call a pro before mold starts. Within 24 to 48 hours, mold growth can get underway. Once it does, the job gets longer and more expensive.
  • Move what you can. Get rugs, furniture and boxes off wet floors if it is safe.

Do not walk through standing water if the power is on, and stay out of anything that may contain sewage. The risk of being shocked or getting sick outweighs anything you might save. If the water came from outside, leave flood cleanup to a crew with the right protective gear.

Getting a Real Number

National ranges are useful for planning, but your home is not an average. An inspection with a moisture meter shows how far the water travelled and what it will take to dry and repair.

Damage only gets bigger with time, so call for restoration of water damage in Yonkers, NY and have the inspection done now.
