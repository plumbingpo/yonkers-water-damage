A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Yonkers, Yonkers Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • A backwater valve at the sewer connection stops the city system from backing up into your home
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Have your sewer line inspected with a camera every 2 to 3 years to catch potential problems

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.
