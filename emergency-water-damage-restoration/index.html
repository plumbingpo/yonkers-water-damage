Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.

Why Emergency Water Damage Restoration Is Not a DIY Job

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. The difficulty lies in tracking where the remaining water travelled. Water runs under baseboards, into wall cavities and beneath flooring within minutes. With a thermal imaging camera and moisture meters we locate that hidden water and treat it the same night, before it turns up weeks later as an odor or a stain.

Truck-mounted extractors, commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers matched to the room come with the crew, and no household keeps those around. The crew is trained to stay safe near contaminated water and wet wiring, and from hour one it records the loss in photos and moisture readings.
