How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Rooms that start to smell stale or moldy following a storm

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
