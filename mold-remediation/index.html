Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Laundry rooms, baths and kitchens all need working exhaust ventilation that is sized for the space
  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Dry wet rugs, towels and spills right away instead of letting them sit

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Recent water damage or flooding that was not professionally dried
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Mold or mildew that comes back soon after you clean it

Why Professional Mold Remediation Matters

This is not a job to take on yourself. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. If the moisture is left alone, taking away what you can see only buys time, and regrowth often shows up within weeks. Finding concealed moisture, drying it properly and preventing a repeat all take equipment and training, which certified remediation technicians have.
